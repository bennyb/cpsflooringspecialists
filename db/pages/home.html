Domestic & Commercial Flooring Specialists

CPS FLOORING SPECIALISTS has over 20 years experience within the flooring industry, supplying and installing both commercial and domestic flooring across Kent, Surrey, London and the surrounding areas. We offer an affordable service to suit all budgets without compromising the end result, no job is too big - no job is too small. We can assist you with any current or future projects you may have and always come with the highest level of customer care and satisfaction.

We offer a huge range of various products and we provide you with samples and site surveys to your front door or commercial premises.
